Our story

It started with a real gap

Pro House was founded by a bodybuilder who struggled to find clean, high quality meals at a fair price.

Tired of low standards and expensive options, he created Pro House to deliver what was missing — real healthy food, made right, and priced for consistency.

Our mission is to make clean, high quality nutrition accessible to everyone. We are committed to serving fresh, balanced, and performance driven meals that fuel the body without compromising on taste, quality, or affordability.
